Not a video · 不是视频

Every frame you see is drawn live in your browser. Scroll down and the camera circles the piece; scroll back and it turns the other way.

你看到的每一帧都是浏览器实时画出来的。往下滚,镜头绕着作品转;往回滚,倒着转。

From photographs · 从照片到三维

The photographer walked twice around the display case. Software recovered where each photograph was taken, then rebuilt the piece as hundreds of thousands of tiny coloured light points — Gaussian splatting.

摄影师绕展柜走了两圈。程序先算出每张照片的拍摄位置,再把作品还原成几十万个带颜色的微小光点,这就是"高斯泼溅"。
